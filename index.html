Comparative HGIS Methodology & Relational Model

Drawing on the 1649–1650 (H. 1059–1060) separation of Şikâyet Defterleri from the Mühimme Defterleri, this explorer extends the three core archival tables (petitioners_table, locations_table, receiver_table) with four relational schemas keyed by decree_id: Defendants (Şikâyet Edilenler), Financial Claims (Parasal Miktarlar), Administrative Hierarchy (Eyalet–Sancak–Kaza–Karye), and Imperial Directives (Dîvân Kararları).

Place-name standardization and sancak/eyalet assignments cross-reference Kaynaklarıyla Osmanlı Coğrafyası Yer Adları Sözlüğü alongside the spatial frameworks of Mapping Ottoman Epirus (MapOE) and The 1832/33 Ottoman Property Survey of Cyprus.

Global Petitioning Historiography & Monetary Notes

By mapping non-Muslim subjects (dhimmi), women (e.g., Ayşe Hatun #2, Mümine #18, Fatma Hatun #25), collective villages, and provincial officials relative to imperial courier routes, the dataset brings Ottoman empirical evidence into dialogue with early modern petitioning scholarship (Brodie Waddell, David Zaret, Richard Huzzey).

Currency Note: Explicit sums from the register (e.g., 6,330 guruş in Decree #27; 2,200 guruş in Decree #30; 4 akçe/unit customs fee in Decree #21) are recorded verbatim and distinguished from unquantified qualitative claims (marked Unquantified / Gayr-ı Muharrer) or standardized archival conversions (1 esedî guruş = 80 akçe).
