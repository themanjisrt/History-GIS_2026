NOTES

Archival Methodology & Territorial Framework

Historical GIS Grounding

The 1649 Şikâyet Defterleri Split

In 1649 (H. 1059), the Ottoman imperial chancery separated petition-driven decrees from the general Mühimme Defterleri, creating the dedicated Şikâyet Defterleri series. Register No. 1 contains 860 decrees across the empire; this dashboard examines the opening 1–45 folio slice (39 recorded decrees, 49 geocoded sites).

Toponymic Normalization

Ottoman provincial administration nested nahiye and kaza districts under sancak (liva) and eyalet provinces. Multi-site decrees—such as Haleb–Kilis–Azez (Decrees 1, 34) and İzmir–Manisa–Kütahya–Aydın (Decree 35)—are standardized using Kaynaklarıyla Osmanlı Coğrafyası Yer Adları Sözlüğü.

Practical Agency & Global Historiography

While theoretical law granted every subject—Muslim or non-Muslim, woman or villager—the right to petition Istanbul, spatial mapping tests practical access. Notice how Decrees 3, 4, 13, 26, 44, and 45 are absent from this excerpted sheet, and Decree 43 (Seydişehri) lacks a geocoded row in the exported locations slice.
